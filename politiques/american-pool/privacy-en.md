Android · `com.ajsstudio.americanpool`

## Playing on your phone

Local games do not require an account. Profiles, settings, progress, journals and saved games stay in app storage. Android system backups follow your settings; private access credentials and sign-in sessions are excluded. Exported JSON files remain under your control.

## Optional Google sign-in and backup

Google and Firebase Authentication handle your account ID, name and email address. Signing in does not automatically upload profiles. Uploading or restoring a Firestore backup requires confirmation. A restore retains a local recovery copy and protects active games.

## Clubs and online games

Firebase Realtime Database stores nicknames, availability, memberships, invitations, shots and results needed for clubs and friendly games. Relevant members or opponents can see shared information. Directory-listed clubs expose information according to their visibility setting. Advanced private-server games use a separate server selected by the host.

## Advertising

Starting with the ad-supported version 0.29.0, the app uses Google AdMob for ads and Google UMP to manage your choices. Google may process the IP address to estimate approximate location, interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required, use Advertising privacy in the settings to change your choices. Version 0.28.0 is ad-free.

## Retention and deletion

Local profiles remain until deleted or app data is cleared. A cloud backup remains until replaced or deleted. Invitations expire, but expiry does not purge every historical record. Shared game history and opponent nicknames may remain. Account deletion removes the private data covered by the deletion flow; a minimal technical closure marker remains to prevent old access from recreating the backup. No automatic purge of every game history is promised.

## Providers and requests

Google provides Firebase, AdMob and UMP. Its data processing and transfers are subject to its terms and privacy policy. GitHub hosts this public notice. Contact the publisher to request access, correction or deletion of your data. Identity verification may be required. Never send a password or token.
