Blackball privacy

Updated 16 November 2026. This notice describes the game and its optional services. Internal builds 12204 and 12205 use demonstration ads; launch build 12206 provides for commercial ads, subject to availability and privacy choices.

Age and offline mode

On first launch, a neutral question asks your age. Only an age band is stored on this device, outside Android backup allowlists: neither exact age nor date of birth is retained or sent. Users under 16 and those who do not provide an age use offline mode: local profiles, computer matches, exercises and Arcade, without AdMob/UMP requests, Google sign-in, Firebase backup or PVP. Uninstalling may erase local data; Android system backups depend on device settings. Optional services below are available from age 16. This is self-declared age, not identity verification or parental consent collection.

On your device

Profiles, settings, results, practice records and shot journals are stored on the phone. The journal contains the last 200 shots per profile. The coach runs on the device without an external AI service.

Sign-in and backup

From age 16, Google sign-in is optional for local features. Google and Firebase Authentication process account ID, name and email address. A Firebase backup includes profiles, results, settings and journals in the export. Manual uploads are voluntary. Automatic backup requires activation for the selected Google account; it runs while the app is open and backup conditions are met, rather than as a permanent background service. Restoration or account changes should not silently overwrite conflicting data.

Matches and clubs

Firebase Realtime Database processes account IDs, nicknames, clubs and memberships, presence, invitations, shots and shared results. Club members see nicknames and presence; match participants see match data. Email addresses are not added to the member list. The block list is private to the account.

Advertising and choices

The launch version may display Google AdMob ads. Delivery depends on applicable consent, your age band and ad availability. For users aged 16 and over, Google UMP collects applicable advertising choices before permitted AdMob initialisation and ad requests. Choices are available under Settings → Information → Advertising and privacy. Ages 16–17 receive Google teen advertising treatment; creatives are limited to rating G. Banners appear on eligible menus; ads do not appear on the game table, during the official timer or PVP. Interstitials are frequency-limited and offered after an action on a completed result.

Even with test ads, the SDKs may process technical data: IP addresses that may indicate approximate location, interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers. Google describes their use for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising SDK transfers use TLS. Offline mode described above makes no ad or UMP form requests. Google Mobile Ads.

Retention and deletion

Local profiles and results remain on your device until deleted or app data is cleared. JSON exports you keep yourself must be deleted separately. Cloud backups are retained to allow restoration while your account remains open, until replaced or deleted. Club and match data support online play and match history; they remain until the relevant deletion or account closure. No scheduled purge of inactive accounts or old matches is deployed.

Closure retains technical identifier and status markers to prevent an old session from rewriting data during cleanup. These are not copies of your profiles. Support requests are kept while being handled; additional retention may be needed to document the response or deal with a dispute, limited to necessary information and the applicable period. Technical data processed by Google is also subject to that provider’s retention practices, linked below.

What account deletion removes

From game settings, deletion requires Google reauthentication, removes the cloud backup, cleans up private data, memberships and invitations accessible to the account, then deletes the Firebase identity. From build 12205, online matches in which the account participated and their entries in both players’ online histories are also removed. If cleanup fails, the flow can be retried; identity deletion waits for these steps to succeed.

Phone profiles and personal exports remain in your possession. Residual references, including block lists or older invitations, may require support intervention: use the deletion page to request their erasure, or deletion of selected data without closing your account. Your Google account itself is not deleted.

Why we process these data

Account, backup and online gameplay processing is necessary to provide the service you request. Account data is needed for these features, but sign-in remains optional for local play. Account security and abuse prevention rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the service. Handling your data-rights requests fulfils our legal obligations. Advertising processing requiring consent relies on your Google form choices, which you can change in settings. In applicable US states, a message lets users opt out of advertising-related sale or sharing of their data. The game coach makes no decisions producing legal effects on you.

Your rights and requests

You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing and, where applicable, exercise rights to object and data portability. Consent may be withdrawn for the future without affecting earlier lawful processing. An Android permission does not necessarily constitute consent to advertising use.

Specify the application and data concerned. Where necessary, we will request only the information needed to verify account ownership. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Requests must receive a response without undue delay, normally within one month. An extension of two months is possible for complex or numerous requests, with notice within the first month. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Providers and transfers

When using the Google/Firebase features described on this page, these providers process information needed for the service, including IP addresses and technical connection data. A European database location does not guarantee all processing stays in Europe. See Firebase privacy and transfer information and Google’s privacy policy.

The website is hosted by OVHcloud. Email requests are handled by AJS Studio and the mail services needed to deliver them. Website information is described separately in the website privacy notice.
