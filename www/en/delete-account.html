AJS STUDIO · YOUR RIGHTS

Request
deletion.

Direct access, even without the app. Choose the game to prepare your email.

You may request deletion of the online account, backup or other data about you.

Prepare my email ↗

This opens your email app. Nothing is sent by this website and no data is deleted automatically: review and send your email. Without an email app, write directly to contact@ajsstudio.fr.

What to include

  • The application name.
  • The account concerned: sign-in email and, if known, nickname or game identifier.
  • Your request: account and associated data deletion, backup-only deletion or other specified data.

Do not send a password, sign-in code or backup containing other players’ data.

What happens next

AJS Studio reviews the request and may verify your identity proportionately. The response explains which data was removed and any data retained, with its reason and applicable duration. Sending an email is not confirmation of deletion.

Online account and phone

For Blackball and Snooker, in-app flows can delete the game account and its backup while retaining local profiles and some markers or history. Each game notice describes the limits. For Carom By AJS Studio, the game’s privacy notice explains how to delete the account and cloud backup. For American Pool and Dart Club, distinguish phone profiles from any private-server match records. Exports and copies on your devices remain under your control.

Clearing local data in Android resets the app and may permanently remove progress. That action alone does not delete a cloud account or exported backup.

Your contact

Thomas Sauvé, sole trader operating as AJS Studio, 33 rue de la Vierge Noire, 29600 Morlaix, France. Questions and data requests: contact@ajsstudio.fr.

Your rights and requests

You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing and, where applicable, exercise rights to object and data portability. Consent may be withdrawn for the future without affecting earlier lawful processing. An Android permission does not necessarily constitute consent to advertising use.

Specify the application and data concerned. Where necessary, we will request only the information needed to verify account ownership. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Requests must receive a response without undue delay, normally within one month. An extension of two months is possible for complex or numerous requests, with notice within the first month. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Providers and transfers

When using the Google/Firebase features described on this page, these providers process information needed for the service, including IP addresses and technical connection data. A European database location does not guarantee all processing stays in Europe. See Firebase privacy and transfer information and Google’s privacy policy.

The website is hosted by OVHcloud. Email requests are handled by AJS Studio and the mail services needed to deliver them. Website information is described separately in the website privacy notice.
