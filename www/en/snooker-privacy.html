Snooker By AJS Studio

Your data, your choices.

Local profilesSingle-profile backupDedicated Firebase
Public versions

This notice describes Snooker By AJS Studio. The Advertising section applies from version 0.21.5.

Local data

Profiles, matches, frames, statistics, Arcade progress and practice records are stored on the phone. Offline play does not require a Google account.

Account and backup

Optional sign-in uses Google and Firebase Authentication: identifier, name and email address. The player explicitly links a profile. The Firestore backup covers that profile, its results and progress, including participation data needed for history. Restoring it preserves other profiles on the phone.

Clubs and online matches

A dedicated snooker Firebase project handles authentication, backups, clubs, invitations, presence and shot synchronisation. Data needed for a match is shared with its participants. The service does not use the Blackball data space.

Advertising

From version 0.21.5, Google AdMob provides banner ads in the menus and ads shown when you return to the home screen after some completed local matches.

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process and share information such as IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available. See how Google uses this information.

Where consent is required, the game offers options to consent, decline or manage your choices. You can change your choices or withdraw consent via Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is available.

Retention and deletion

Local data and exported copies are separate from the online backup. Account deletion reauthenticates the Google account, cleans up the relevant online data and removes the backup and Firebase identity. Local profiles remain. Private closure markers prevent reuse of old tokens. Retention periods for shared history, markers and service logs still need to be specified before public distribution.

Requests must receive a response without undue delay, normally within one month. An extension of two months is possible for complex or numerous requests, with notice within the first month. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Providers and transfers

When using the Google/Firebase features described on this page, these providers process information needed for the service, including IP addresses and technical connection data. A European database location does not guarantee all processing stays in Europe. See Firebase privacy and transfer information and Google’s privacy policy.

The website is hosted by OVHcloud. Email requests are handled by AJS Studio and the mail services needed to deliver them. Website information is described separately in the website privacy notice.
